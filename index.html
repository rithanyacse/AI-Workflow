System Logic: If the water quality is good, the green LED turns ON and the tap is unlocked. If contaminated water is detected, the red LED turns ON, the tap is locked and the 3-way valve drains the dirty water. The water-level sensor detects when the tank becomes empty. Fresh water is then allowed to enter and the purifier refills the tank. The water quality is checked again until the water becomes safe.
